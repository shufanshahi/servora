  other agency.
- **CC BY 2.0**: reuse is allowed with credit to the creator, a link to the licence, and an
  indication that changes were made. The table and the paragraph above provide all three.
- **CC BY-SA 4.0** (`swedish-massage.jpg` and `garden-care.jpg`): as above, and the *modified image
  file itself* must also be shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. This applies to these two image files only.
  It does not affect the licence of the rest of this repository's source code.

If you are the creator of one of these photographs and want it credited differently or removed,
open an issue and it will be changed promptly.
